// WHAT YOU'RE SEEING

MARKET VIEW · HOW THE TIME TUNNEL WORKS

  • Into the past: you look down the time tunnel, walking backwards through time. NOW is the outer edge; older trades sit deeper, shrinking into the PAST.
  • One loop of the coil = the zoom window (15–60s); the trail fades into the far PAST ring at the window's end. The rings are marked −5s, −10s, −15s… back to the window's end. Zoom stretches the PAST, never NOW.
  • Price: a point pushed outward from its loop traded higher than the latest trade, inward = lower. Small moves are softened; a sudden big move breaks out wide and flashes white-hot.
  • Colour = who hit the market: green = buyers, red = sellers, blends = mixed. Thicker, brighter = more volume. Flares = the most-traded prices of the last few minutes.
  • Tunnel walls: the lines running inward carry you from NOW at the edge to the PAST at the glowing far ring.
  • Hub ring = traffic: a slow, soft pulse at normal trading; busier than the 10-minute norm, it pulses faster and glows harder, with a corona around the hub when it's very busy or bursting. Its colour follows the diamond (buyers / sellers / balanced).
  • NOW needle: your position in time. It turns once a minute (the hub's dashes turn with it), and price slides the head along it: out toward the edge = price up, in toward the hub = price down (mid-way = the recent average). The average and bands land on it too.
  • The head glows green on up moves and red on down moves; a fast move leaves a short glowing wake in the same colour.
  • Yellow line = moving average (~window/6). Violet bands = Bollinger bands (±2 standard deviations): narrow = squeeze, wide = expansion. Each has an on/off switch in TECH.
  • A small ring flashes when price crosses the average; sparks glint where the average and bands meet the NOW needle.
  • Diamond on the outer coloured arc = last 30s of market buys vs sells: left/green = buyers in control, right/red = sellers, top/yellow = balanced.
  • “continued 42 of the last 100 moves” (small grey text by the volume strip): how often a pattern's move kept going over the next minute, out of its last 100 clear moves. Neutral moves (smaller than the spread) are left out. Past only; shown only when clearly off a coin-flip. (all coins) = this coin hasn't had 100 clear moves yet.
  • RSI arc (bottom): RSI 14 on 1-minute candles; left = oversold (<30), right = overbought (>70).
  • Zoom: AUTO picks the window from traffic. Swipe up = zoom in (shorter), down = out; pinch, wheel or the zoom bar also work. A manual zoom turns AUTO off; double-tap or ZOOM ▸ AUTO turns it back on.

MARKET · SIDE VIEW

  • The time tunnel turned on its side: time runs left to right, from the PAST ring to NOW at the front dial's ring.
  • Height = price. The line is the volume-weighted price of real trades from all five exchanges (or the one picked in SRC), lightly smoothed (~2 s): green where buyers dominated, pink where sellers did, a little thicker where volume was heavier. History from before the page opened has no buy/sell split yet, so it shows as a plain cyan line.
  • The faint band behind the line is the true range: every slice's real high and low (smoothed into a band), so outlier prints stay visible without spiking the line.
  • Rings mark equal time steps (5M: every minute, 15M: every 5 min, 60M: every 15 min), labelled in a row along the tube's bottom edge.
  • 5M / 15M / 60M picks how much history the tunnel shows. Average, bands, diamond and hub work as in the front view.
  • The SIDE ▸ / FRONT ▸ button swaps between the two views. Turning to SIDE, the buyers / sellers arc rides down the tunnel to the PAST ring; turning back, it rides up to the front ring.

LIMIT VIEW · ORDER-BOOK WALLS

  • Arcs are walls of waiting orders: buyers left, sellers right.
  • Distance from the middle = distance from the current price.
  • Bigger, brighter arc = bigger wall.
  • Price zoom (bar opposite the time bar, ±0.15%–±1%): out shows walls further from price, in spreads the near ones. Phone: swipe ←/→ for price, ↑/↓ for time. Desktop: shift+wheel or the bar. AUTO follows traffic, like time AUTO: dead-slow ±0.15%, normal ±0.5%, busy or a burst ±1%, in clean steps (±0.15/0.25/0.5/0.75/1%; the white halfway circle's boxes show price and ±% there); zooming price by hand turns price AUTO off.
  • New walls start at the center and drift outward over time. A wall that stays is holding; one that vanishes was pulled or eaten.
  • SUPPORT / RESISTANCE = the strongest nearby wall on each side.
  • EATEN (bright bite flash) = a wall shrank while trades hit its price. PULLED (ghostly dashed outline) = it vanished with little trading there; repeat pulls in one zone = SPOOF? Just a hint: intent can't be proven.

AUTO ZOOM

  • MARKET: normal traffic shows ~30s, dead-slow up to 60s. Price moves tighten it; volume bursts snap to a tight 15s time tunnel.
  • LIMIT: the reverse, same traffic measure: dead-slow = 15s, normal ~30s, twice normal or a burst zooms out to 1m so you see walls get hit.
  • AUTO off = full zoom out; then zoom by swipe up/down (both views; pinch also works in MARKET), wheel or the zoom bar. In LIMIT the AUTO button drives both time and price zoom.
  • Needs ~2 min after load to learn normal traffic.
