// WHAT YOU'RE SEEING

MARKET VIEW · LIVE TRADES

  • Every trade lands on a spiral: newest on the outer ring, older ones wind into the center.
  • One lap = the time window (15–60s, shown on the zoom bar).
  • Trail swings out = price up, in = price down.
  • Green = buyers, red = sellers. Thicker trail = more volume.
  • Flares mark the spots with the heaviest trading.

LIMIT VIEW · ORDER-BOOK WALLS

  • Arcs are walls of waiting orders: buyers left, sellers right.
  • Distance from the middle = distance from the current price.
  • Bigger, brighter arc = bigger wall.
  • New walls start at the center and drift outward over time. A wall that stays is holding; one that vanishes was pulled or eaten.
  • SUPPORT / RESISTANCE = the strongest nearby wall on each side.
  • EATEN (bright bite flash) = a wall shrank while trades hit its price. PULLED (ghostly dashed outline) = it vanished with little trading there; repeat pulls in one zone = SPOOF? Just a hint: intent can't be proven.

AUTO ZOOM

  • MARKET: normal traffic shows ~30s, dead-slow up to 60s. Price moves tighten it; volume bursts snap to a 15s vortex.
  • LIMIT: quiet = 15s; heavy trading zooms out to 1m so you see walls get hit.
  • AUTO off = full zoom out; then zoom by pinch, wheel or the zoom bar.

ALSO

  • RSI arc (bottom): momentum. Left = oversold, right = overbought.
  • SRC: venues feeding the book. Pick ALL or one exchange. Each exchange merges its USD pair with its USDT / USDC / RLUSD pairs where listed (chips show which are live). Stablecoin prices are converted to USD at that exchange's live stable/USD rate; a quote more than ~0.3% off-peg is left out.
  • COIN: switch between BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL and other big coins (USD and stablecoin pairs from all five exchanges). Your pick is remembered; links can use ?coin=ETH.
